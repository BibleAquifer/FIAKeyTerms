## queen (id: eng-t311-v1)

A **queen** is usually the wife of a king. A queen is usually of lower status than the king, but higher status than everyone else. Sometimes, a queen might be the daughter of a king, and that daughter might rule her country after her father dies if her father had no son to become king after him. In biblical times, some queens had a lot of power, while other queens did not have much power.
