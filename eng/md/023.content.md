## Zion (id: eng-t204-v1)

People sometimes used **Zion** as another name for the city of [Jerusalem](#t76). Zion was the name of one of the mountains in the city of Jerusalem, where the people called the Jebusites lived before [King David](#t33) conquered the city. When King David conquered the city, people called the city either "the city of David," or "Zion." Later, people used Zion as a name for the whole city of Jerusalem. Many writers in the Bible use the name Zion especially when they are talking about Jerusalem as a [holy](#t67) place where [God](#t189) lives with his people.

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Kings 8:1-11; 1 Chronicles 11:1-9; Psalms 3:1-8; Matthew 21:1-11

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Jerusalem (ID: `place:Jerusalem`)
