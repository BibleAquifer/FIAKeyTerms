## nation (id: tpi-t94-v1)

Wanpela **nesen** em i wanpela lain manmeri we i stap wantaim na i save stap insait long seim kantri, na husat i gat seim rula o seim man o meri i bosim ol.

Taim Baibel i toktok long "olgeta nesen bilong graun," dispela em i narapela we bilong tok "olgeta manmeri husat i stap long graun."

## nazareth (id: tpi-t95-v1)

**Nasaret** em i wanpela liklik taun insait long rijen bilong [Galili](#t55), long hap not bilong [Israel](#t74). Nasaret i no kamap insait long Olpela Testamen, na namba wan taim yumi harim nem bilong dispela taun em taim yumi harim olsem [Maria](#t89) na Josep, tupela papamama bilong Jisas, i bilong Nasaret. Jisas i bon long wanpela liklik ples ol i kolim [Betlehem](#t13), long [Judea](#t81), tasol taim em i liklik yet, Maria na Josep i bin go stap long Nasaret. Olsem na Jisas bihain ol i kolim em olsem Nasarin, we i min olsem wanpela man bilong Nasaret. Dispela em i no wanpela gutpela tok. **Ol Ju** bilong Judea i save luk daun long ol manmeri bilong Galili, na ol manmeri insait long Galili i save luk daun long ol manmeri bilong Nasaret, bikos em i wanpela liklik taun na em i no impoten.

* **Associated Passages:** Matthew 21:1-11; Matthew 26:69-75; Mark 1:21-28; Mark 6:1-6; Mark 10:46-52; Mark 14:66-72; Mark 16:1-8; Luke 1:26-38; Luke 1:39-56; Luke 2:22-40; Luke 2:41-52; Luke 4:14-30; Luke 4:31-44; John 19:17-30; Acts 3:1-10; Acts 24:1-9; Acts 26:1-23
