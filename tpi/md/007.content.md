## gabriel (id: tpi-t54-v1)

**Gebriel** em i nem bilong wanpela akenjel. Dispela em i wanpela [akenjel](#t4) we i gat spesel [atoriti](#t8). Baibel i kolim tupela akenjel nem: Gabriel na Maikel. Insait long Olpela Testamen, Gebriel i bin tokim Daniel mining bilong **visen** bilong Daniel. Insait long Nupela Testamen, Gebriel i kamap long Sakaraia long tokim em olsem meri bilong em bai karim wanpela pikinini man, maski em i lapun pinis. Dispela pikinini bai kamap **Jon Bilong Baptais**. Sotpela taim bihain long dispela, Gebriel i kamap long yangpela vejin [Maria](#t89) na tokim em olsem em bai gat bel long pawa bilong God. Dispela pikinini man we em bai karim em bai stap king oltaim, na em i dispela [pikinini bilong God](#t129).

* **Associated Passages:** Luke 1:26-38; Luke 1:39-56

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Gebriel (ID: `deity:Gabriel`)

## galilee (id: tpi-t55-v1)

**Galili** em i nem bilong wanpela rijen long noten hap bilong [Israel](#t74). Jisas i bikpela long Galili na em i mekim planti wok bilong autim tok long Galili. Planti bilong **ol disaipel** bilong Jisas i kam long Galili. Planti bilong ol hap long Galili i go antap na kam daun na i gat ol liklik maunten. Long hap ist, o long rait sait bilong Galili em wanpela bikpela raunwara we ol i kolim [Raunwara Galili](#t121). Planti bilong ol manmeri long Galili i mekim mani o painim kaikai bilong ol taim ol i kisim pis na salim pis. Sampela bilong ol disaipel bilong Jisas em ol fisamen husat i stap long ol liklik ples raunim Raunwara Galili. Ol narapela nem bilong Raunwara Galili em **Raunwara bilong Genasaret**, o **Raunwara Taiberius**.

Long taim bilong Jisas, Israel i no bin wanpela indipenden [nesen](#t94). **Ol Israel** em ol Rom i bin bosim ol. Ol Rom i brukim Israel i go long sampela rijen, o provins. Long laip bilong Jisas, wanpela rula nem bilong em Antipas em i bin gavenim Galili, na gavman bilong Rom i bin makim em. Antipas em i pikinini man bilong king Herod, dispela king husat i bin traim long kilim Jisas taim em i bin wanpela bebi. Antipas em i brata bilong Herod, dispela king husat i bin odarim ol lain long krusifaim Jisas.

Ol narapela provins long Israel em [Judea](#t81) na [Samaria](#t221). Galili em i bin long hap not, Judea em bin long hap saut, na Samaria em i bin long namel. Judea em i bin ples we nambawan impoten siti [Jerusalem](#t76), i stap, na [tempel](#t138) bilong God tu i stap long hap. Ol manmeri long Judea planti taim i ting olsem ol i gutpela **Ju o Judaman** moa long ol manmeri long Galili. Ol i save luk daun long ol manmeri long Galili, na ting olsem ol manmeri long Galili i no gat save na i raf lain. Ol manmeri long Judea na tu long Galili, i bin save luk daun long ol manmeri long Samaria, we ol i lukim ol olsem ol i no Ju.

* **Associated Passages:** Joshua 21:27-33; 1 Kings 9:10-25; Matthew 10:1-15; Matthew 17:22-27; Matthew 19:1-12; Matthew 21:1-11; Matthew 26:69-75; Matthew 28:1-15; Matthew 28:16-20; Mark 1:14-20; Mark 1:21-28; Mark 1:35-39; Mark 6:14-29; Mark 8:27-30; Mark 9:30-50; Mark 14:27-31; Mark 14:66-72; Mark 16:1-8; Luke 1:1-4; Luke 1:26-38; Luke 3:1-14; Luke 4:14-30; Luke 8:26-39; Luke 8:40-56; Luke 10:1-16; Luke 13:1-9; Luke 24:1-12; John 4:1-15; John 4:43-54; John 7:1-10; Acts 1:6-11; Acts 3:1-10

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Galili (ID: `place:Galilee`); Judia (ID: `place:Judea`); Galileans (ID: `group:Galilee`)

## genealogy (id: tpi-t172-v1)

Wanpela **jinioloji** em i wanpela list bilong nem bilong ol manmeri husat i kam long seim tumbuna. Wanpela jinioloji em i ken stat wantaim wanpela man na bihain givim wanpela list bilong ol **tumbuna pikinini** bilong dispela man\-ol lain manmeri we i kam bihain long em\-o em i ken stat wantaim wanpela man na givim list bilong ol **tumbuna papamama**\-ol manmeri husat i bin stap bipo long em.

Wanpela jinioloji i ken karamapim planti [jeneresen](#t173) long longpela hap taim. Sampela taim list i save skipim sampela manmeri husat i bin stap insait long dispela famili, na givim ol nambawan impoten nem tasol. Planti taim yumi harim nem bilong ol man, tasol insait long Baibel i gat sampela jinioloji we yumi harim nem bilong ol meri tu. Sampela taim stori bilong wanpela man o meri i save stat wantaim wanpela jinioloji.

* **Associated Passages:** Genesis 11:27-32; Genesis 25:12-18; Genesis 25:19-34; 1 Chronicles 1:28-33; 1 Chronicles 4:24-43; 1 Chronicles 5:1-10; 1 Chronicles 5:11-17; 1 Chronicles 7:6-13; 1 Chronicles 7:30-40; 1 Chronicles 8:17-28; 1 Chronicles 9:1-9; 1 Chronicles 9:17-27; 1 Chronicles 9:28-34; 1 Chronicles 26:29-32; 2 Chronicles 12:1-16; 2 Chronicles 31:11-21; Ezra 2:59-63; Nehemiah 7:5-25; 1 Timothy 1:3-7; Titus 3:1-11


## generations (id: tpi-t173-v1)

Wanpela **jeneresen** em i toktok long wanpela lain manmeri husat i stap long seim taim na i bilong wanpela seim eij grup. Insait long wanpela komyuniti, tripela jeneresen bilong ol manmeri i ken stap long seim taim: ol tumbuna papamama, ol papamama, na ol pikinini. Wanwan bilong ol dispela lain i bilong wanpela narapela jeneresen. 

Sampela taim God, o Jisas, i toktok long "manmeri bilong dispela jeneresen." Dispela em i min olgeta manmeri husat i stap laip long wanpela hap taim. 

Insait long Olpela Testamen, dispela tok "ol dispela em ol jeneresen bilong dispela man o meri..." em ol bin save yusim long introdusim ol stori bilong dispela man o meri. Olsem na, em i min olsem "dispela em i histori bilong dispela man o meri."

## gentile (id: tpi-t56-v1)

Dispela wod **Jentail** em i kam long wanpela wod we i min "manmeri" o "**ol nesen**."

Insait long Olpela Testamen, God i bin makim **ol Israel** long stap olsem ol spesel manmeri bilong em. Ol Israel i bin nid long stap seperet long ol manmeri bilong ol narapela nesen na bai ol Israel i sanap strong long bihainim God. Ol i mas no ken stat long [wosipim](#t149) **ol aidol** bilong ol narapela nesen na bihainim ol pasin nogut bilong ol. Tasol, taim God i bin mekim [kavanen](#t29) bilong em wantaim [Abraham](#t1), dispela nambawan tumbuna bilong ol Israel, God i bin tok tu olsem long ol lain tumbuna bilong Abraham **olgeta** manmeri bilong wol bai i kisim **blesing**. God i laikim olgeta manmeri, i no ol Israel tasol, husat ol i save kolim olsem **Ju o Judaman**.

Bihain long sampela taim, ol Israel i ting ol i antap moa long ol narapela lain bikos long spesel posisen bilong ol na i luk daun long ol manmeri husat i no Ju. Dispela wod Jentail em i stat long minim samting negativ\-wanpela man o meri nogut, wanpela man o meri we yu mas stap longwe long en. Long taim bilong Jisas, ol Rom i bin rulim kantri Israel. Olsem na planti manmeri husat i no Ju em ol tu i stap long Israel. Olgeta bilong ol dispela manmeri em ol i kolim ol Jentail. Ol Ju i nid long wok wantaim ol Jentail, tasol ol i no save poro poro wantaim ol. Ol Ju i tambuim tru ol Jentail long go insait long [tempel](#t138) eria, na ol Ju i no save kaikai wantaim ol Jentail o go insait long haus bilong wanpela Jentail. Ol Ju i **bin bilip** olsem pasin bilong intarek o stap wantaim ol Jentail i bai mekim ol "**i no klin**"\-o i no fit long wosipim God.

Sampela taim, yumi no painim wod Jentail, yumi painim wod "**Grik**." Wanpela Grik em i wanpela man o meri husat i bilong kantri Gris, tasol bihain long sampela taim, dispela wod i stat long minim ol man o meri husat i adoptim na bihainim Grik kalsa. Wanpela Ju i no inap long adoptim o bihainim Grik kalsa, we i gat pasin bilong wosipim aidol. Dispela wod Grik em tu ol i stat long yusim long ol wanpela man o meri husat i no wanpela Ju, maski dispela man o meri em i no bilong kantri Gris. Olsem na dispela wod "Grik" na "Jentail" i ken sampela taim minim seim samting.

* **Associated Passages:** Psalms 117:1-2; Matthew 4:12-25; Matthew 5:43-48; Matthew 6:1-8; Matthew 6:19-34; Matthew 8:1-17; Matthew 10:1-15; Matthew 10:16-25; Matthew 12:15-21; Matthew 18:15-20; Matthew 20:17-28; Mark 7:24-30; Mark 10:32-45; Luke 18:31-34; Luke 21:20-28; Luke 22:24-38; John 7:25-36; Acts 4:23-31; Acts 9:1-19; Acts 10:23-33; Acts 10:34-48; Acts 11:1-18; Acts 11:19-26; Acts 13:13-22; Acts 13:23-41; Acts 13:42-52; Acts 14:1-7; Acts 15:1-21; Acts 15:22-35; Acts 18:1-17; Acts 21:10-14; Acts 21:15-26; Acts 21:27-36; Acts 21:37-22:21; Acts 26:1-23; Acts 28:17-31; 1 Corinthians 1:18-25; 1 Corinthians 12:12-26; Galatians 1:11-24; Galatians 2:1-10; Galatians 2:11-14; Galatians 2:15-21; Galatians 3:1-9; Galatians 3:10-14; Galatians 3:26-4:7; Ephesians 1:11-14; Ephesians 2:11-18; Ephesians 2:19-22; Ephesians 3:1-6; Ephesians 4:17-24; 1 Thessalonians 2:14-16; 1 Thessalonians 4:1-8; 1 Timothy 2:1-7; 1 Timothy 3:14-16; 2 Timothy 4:9-22

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Greeks (ID: `group:Greek`); Kenan (ID: `place:Canaan`)

## gerasenes (id: tpi-t57-v2.1)

Gerasins, Geresa, Gadara, Gadarins

Gerasins, Geresa, Gadara, na Gadarins em olgeta narapela nem bilong wanpela eria na ol siti insait long eria we i stap long saut ist koust bilong Raunwara Galili. Planti manmeri husat i stap long hap em ol i no ol Ju o Judaman. Dispela eria em i pat bilong Dekapolis.

* **Associated Passages:** Mark 5:1-20

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Gerasa (ID: `place:Gerasa`)

## glorify (id: tpi-t199-v1)

[Glori](#t58) em i biknem na rispek we yu kisim taim yu mekim wanpela narapela kain samting.

Taim ol manmeri i **givim** glori long God, ol [preisim](#t219) God, rispektim em, na tok ol gutpela samting long God.

Taim God i **soim** glori bilong em, ol manmeri i ken lukim olsem God em i nais tru, i pulap long pawa na strong. I no planti manmeri insait long Baibel i gat sans long lukim glori bilong God, olsem na taim em i kamap, em i wanpela spesel na narapela kain taim tru. Em i narapela kain na ol manmeri i save kamap pret tru taim ol i lukim glori bilong God. Sampela taim ol manmeri i save lukim wanpela brait lait long seim taim, tasol i no olgeta taim.

Em i ken hat long trensletim wod glori. Long sampela taim, yu ken tok "Pawa na lait bilong God we i sain." Taim yu givim glori long God, yu soim pawa na lait bilong God we i sain.

## god (id: tpi-t189-v1)

Insait long Baibel, dispela wod **God** em i toktok long God we i wokim olgeta samting. God em i wanpela spirit we i gat nambawan bikpela pawa tru. Dispela em God husat i mekim wanpela [kavanen](#t29), wanpela spesel promis, long [Abraham](#t1) na ol lain tumbuna bilong em, **ol Israel**. Dispela em God husat i salim Jisas i kam long graun long seivim ol manmeri long dai na [kirap bek](#t110) bilong em.

God em i save stap, na em bai i stap oltaim. Bikos God i wokim olgeta samting, em i gat pawa antap long olgeta samting. God em i gutpela na i pulap long laikim, i pulap long pawa, na i stret tasol.

Taim yu trensletim God, yu mas lukaut long ol dispela samting: Sampela komyuniti i save [wosipim](#t149) planti kainkain spirit. Long sampela taim, nem we ol manmeri i yusim long nambawan spirit we i pulap long pawa em ol i ken yusim long God. Tasol, yu nid long sekim olsem dispela spirit em i no gat planti kainkain pasin we i no wankain olsem God bilong Baibel.

## gods (id: tpi-t235-v1)

Insait long Baibel, dispela wod [God](#t189) em i toktok long [Yahweh](#t174), dispela God husat i wokim olgeta samting, dispela nambawan pawaful spirit. Dispela em God husat i mekim wanpela kavanen, wanpela spesel promis, long [Abraham](#t1) na ol **lain tumbuna** bilong em, **ol Israel**. Dispela em God husat i salim pikinini bilong em Jisas i kam long graun long seivim ol manmeri long panismen bilong ol bikos ol i no harim tok. Yahweh, dispela krieta God, i tokim ol manmeri bilong Israel long no ken wosipim o bihainim ol narapela god, ol i mas bihainim Yahweh tasol. Tasol, sampela taim ol manmeri i wosipim ol narapela spirit o aidol olsem ol i gat pawa. Sampela taim ol manmeri i yusim plang o metel long mekim ol stetyu o longpela pos long makim ol Ol manmeri i save kolim ol dispela stetyu **ol aidol**.

Ol eksampel bilong ol narapela god insait long Olpela Testamen em ol Baal na Astarot bilong ol Kenan, na Dagon, em i god bilong ol Filistain. Insait long Nupela Testamen, sampela manmeri insait long ol ples we Pol i go long en i wosipim ol narapela god, na ol i bildim ol tempel na aidol tu long ol dispela narapela god, wankain olsem ol manmeri insait long Olpela Testamen i bin mekim.

## grace (id: tpi-t61-v1)

Taim wanpela man o meri i givim greis long yumi, ol i mekim gutpela samting long yumi, o ol i givim yumi gutpela samting we yumi no sapos long kisim.

God i save soim greis long ol manmeri taim em i **fogivim** ol long **ol sins** bilong ol, na mekim ol i kamap ol pikinini bilong em. Ol manmeri i mas stap seperet long God bikos ol i sakim tok bilong God, tasol God i laikim ol manmeri tru olsem na em i laik long fogivim ol. Olsem na God i mekim gutpela samting long ol manmeri we ol i no sapos long kisim: Em i fogivim ol. Dispela em greis.

## greeks (id: tpi-t211-v1)

Ol manmeri bilong kantri Gris em ol i save kolim ol olsem **ol Grik**. Gris em i wanpela kantri we i stap long sauten pat bilong eria we nau yumi kolim Yurop. Planti handret yia bipo long Krais i bon, Gris em i bin nambawan pawaful [nesen](#t94) insait long dispela eria, na ol Grik i bin bosim wanpela bikpela hap teritori. Dispela teritori bilong ol em [Israel](#t74) tu i stap insait long en.

Grik kalsa em i gat planti samting bilong ol narapela kantri insait long en. Planti ol manmeri i adoptim ol Grik kastom na stat long toktok long tok ples Grik. Sampela bilong ol i **wosipim** ol Grik god tu. Long dispela taim, planti **ol Ju o Judamanmeri** i stat long ekseptim ol Grik god. Dispela i kamapim wanpela taim bilong woa insait long Israel we ol manmeri husat i laik stap feitful long God i pait agensim ol Grik kastom, na agensim ol manmeri husat i ekseptim ol Grik god.

Bihain long sampela taim, ol manmeri husat i wok long bihainim ol Grik kastom em ol i wok long kolim ol olsem ol Grik maski ol i no bilong kantri Gris.

Long taim bilong Nupela Testamen, ol Grik i bin lusim politikel pawa bilong ol na nau ol Rom i kisim pawa long dispela seim pat bilong wol. Tasol planti manmeri i wok long toktok long tok ples Grik yet. Planti ol manmeri insait long Israel i bin toktok long tok ples bilong ol yet ol i kolim Arameik, tasol ol i toktok long tok ples Grik tu. Ol ota o man i i raitim ol buk bilong Nupela Testamen i bin raitim long Grik tok ples.

Insait long Nupela Testamen, dispela wod Grik em i ken toktok long ol manmeri husat i bilong kantri Gris, ol em i ken toktok tu long ol manmeri husat i bin adoptim na bihainim Grik kalsa na tok ples. Wanpela gutpela Ju o Judaman em i no inap adoptim Grik kalsa we i gat pasin bilong wosipim [aidol](#t72) insait long em, olsem na dispela wod Grik em ol i stat long yusim long minim olsem "wanpela man o meri husat i no Ju." Olsem na sampela taim dispela wod "Grik" em i ken minim seim olsem [Jentail](#t56).
