## zion (id: tpi-t204-v1)

Sampela taim, ol manmeri i save yusim nem **Saion** olsem narapela nem bilong siti [Jerusalem](#t76). Saion em i bin nem bilong wanpela bilong ol maunten insait long siti Jerusalem, ples we ol lain Jebus o Jebusait i bin save stap long en bipo long **King Devit** i holimpasim na kisim dispela siti. Taim King Devit i holimpasim na kisim dispela siti, ol manmeri i bin kolim dispela siti long tupela nem. Ol i save kolim em "Siti bilong Devit," o "Saion." Bihain, ol manmeri i yusim dispela nem Saion olsem wanpela nem bilong olgeta hap bilong siti Jerusalem. Planti ol man husat i raitim ol stori i stap insait long Baibel i yusim nem Saion taim ol i toktok long Jerusalem. Ol i yusim dispela nem taim ol i minim olsem dispela em i wanpela [holi](#t67) ples we [God](#t189) i save stap wantaim ol manmeri bilong em.

* **Associated Passages:** 1 Kings 8:1-11; 1 Chronicles 11:1-9; Psalms 3:1-8; Matthew 21:1-11

* **Associated ACAI Concepts:** Jerusalem (ID: `place:Jerusalem`)
